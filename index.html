FAQ

Questions We Hear
Every Single Day.

We believe in total transparency. No vague answers, no runaround.

How long does it take to build my website?+
Most websites are live within 7–14 days. A simple business site takes 5–7 days; a full e-commerce store takes 2–4 weeks. We always set and honour a clear timeline before we start a single thing.
Is ₦80,000 really all I pay? No hidden fees?+
100% transparent pricing. We quote you everything upfront — design, development, and setup. The only additional costs are your domain (₦5,000–₦15,000/yr) and hosting (₦15,000–₦40,000/yr) — which we help you set up.
Will my website actually show up on Google?+
Every website we build includes foundational SEO — proper structure, meta tags, speed optimisation and Google indexing. For aggressive Google page 1 ranking, our dedicated SEO packages take it much further. Many of our clients rank for their keywords within 30–60 days.
I don't understand technology — will I be able to manage my site?+
Absolutely. We build all websites with simple content management systems so you can update text, images and products yourself — no coding required. We also provide training and written guides. And our support team is always available on WhatsApp.
What if I'm not happy with the design?+
We never start building until you've approved the design. Our process includes a detailed design mockup phase where you give feedback before any development begins. We don't stop until you're genuinely excited about your website.
Can I pay in instalments?+
Yes. We typically work with 60% upfront and 40% on delivery. For our larger packages, we can discuss a flexible payment schedule that works for your business. Just mention it when you reach out.
Do you work with businesses outside Lagos?+
Yes — we work with clients across all of Nigeria (Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan and more) and internationally. Everything is handled remotely with regular video calls, so your location is never a barrier.
